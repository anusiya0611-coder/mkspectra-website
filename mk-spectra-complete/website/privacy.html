Privacy Policy

How MK SPECTRA collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MK SPECTRA ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you visit our website.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us through the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, company name (optional), chosen service, budget range (optional) and message.
  • Usage data, such as pages visited and device or browser type, collected through analytics tools.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or by email.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide our services.
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam.

Third-party services

We use third-party services to run the site, which may process your data: a form service to deliver contact messages, Google Analytics for visitor statistics, and WhatsApp if you choose to message us there. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Our analytics tool may use cookies to measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We keep your details only as long as needed to respond to you and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing mkspectra27@gmail.com.
